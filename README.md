# 📱 DetoxMate – Social Media Detox Tracker App

A wellness-focused mobile application that empowers users to take control of their screen time and develop healthy digital habits using goal setting, gamification, and community support.

---

## 📌 Scenario  
**Scenario 1** – *Social Media Detox Tracker App*  
**Subject:** Design Thinking for Innovation – Year 1  
**Assessment:** Summative Assessment (SA)

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement  
> Many users, especially students and working professionals, struggle to manage their social media usage, leading to anxiety, guilt, and decreased productivity. This app provides a supportive, non-judgmental space to track and reduce screen time in a fun and motivating way.

---

## 🎯 Core Features

- ✅ **Goal Setting**: Set screen time limits and custom detox hours  
- 📊 **Progress Tracker**: View graphs, trends, and app-wise usage insights  
- 🏆 **Gamification**: Earn streaks, badges, and rewards  
- 😌 **Calm Zone**: Access meditation, journaling, and breathing tools  
- 💬 **Community Forum**: Share stories and join group detox challenges  
- 🔍 **Screen Scanner (Bonus)**: Upload screen time screenshots and receive AI suggestions  

---

## 📋 User Research Summary

Based on surveys from 10 individuals (ages 18–30), we found:
- 80% spend over 5 hours/day on social media  
- 90% experience guilt or distraction after long sessions  
- 70% prefer gentle nudges over forced restrictions  
- Most preferred rewards, graphs, and motivational quotes as support tools
